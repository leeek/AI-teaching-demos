ABOUT THE DESIGN

How to read your results

The tool creates profiles for a categorical main effects model. It does not collect responses or estimate preferences.

What do degrees of freedom tell me?

Each attribute needs one fewer parameter than its number of levels. Add those parameters, plus one baseline, to get the total. Residual degrees of freedom equal the number of experimental profiles minus the number of parameters, as long as the model can estimate every main effect. This assumes one complete response per profile for an individual regression.

How should I use the minimum counts?

Use the suggested minimum as your starting point. You can choose a lower count, but it must meet the absolute minimum. The generator may use more profiles to meet the design checks. If there are fewer distinct combinations than the absolute minimum, add attributes or levels. These are course guidelines, not a statistical power calculation.

What does “orthogonal” mean here?

The levels of each pair of attributes occur independently, allowing their main effects to be estimated separately. The tool also checks that every level appears, every profile is distinct, and the model has full rank. Orthogonality does not always require equal counts for every level. The tool may produce different profiles from SPSS and does not guarantee the smallest possible design.

What happens when I exclude profiles?

The design checks update for the remaining profiles. Excluding even one profile can affect orthogonality. If fewer profiles remain than the absolute minimum, or the remaining design cannot estimate all main effects with positive residual degrees of freedom, profile exports and printing are disabled. Review the checks before using an edited design.

Sources and scope

This tool draws on Green and Wind’s conjoint example, IBM’s orthogonal design workflow, and NIST’s factorial design guidance. The example studies use attributes from the supplied class reports; they do not reproduce the reports’ original profile lists.

You can enter 2 to 10 attributes with 2 to 5 levels each and generate up to 128 profiles. Some combinations may not have a design within those limits. This tool does not test interactions, collect responses, estimate utilities, or export SPSS files.
