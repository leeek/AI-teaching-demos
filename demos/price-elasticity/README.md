# Price Elasticity Demo

[Open the demo](./price-elasticity-demo.html)

## What it teaches

This demo illustrates a linear demand curve, price elasticity of demand, and the revenue-maximizing price. Students can change the slope of the demand curve and see how the curve, elasticity regions, revenue-maximizing point, and total revenue respond.

## Course context

This was built for marketing courses that cover pricing, demand, price sensitivity, or revenue optimization. It fits well in a pricing strategy class session or a marketing analytics discussion about how demand and elasticity shape pricing decisions.

## How it was made

Generated with AI as an interactive classroom demo for a business school marketing course. The likely prompt asked for a single-page app demonstrating linear demand, elasticity on hover, and the revenue-maximizing point where elasticity equals -1.
